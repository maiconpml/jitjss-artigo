%% Abstract
\begin{abstract}
The Just-In-Time Job Shop Scheduling problem is a complex scheduling optimization problem where operations possess specific due dates, and completing them outside these deadlines generates earliness or tardiness penalties.
The objective is to minimize the total weighted sum of these penalties.
In this work, we propose a new algorithmic design space parameterized via a context-free grammar in Backus-Naur Form to generate novel metaheuristics for JITJSS automatically.
The configuration process converged to an architecture based on Iterated Tabu Search, validating the relevance of our novel critical-path neighborhood which was selected in 80\% of the elite configurations.
In experimental tests conducted on the literature benchmark instances, the automatically generated algorithm matched or surpassed the best-known values in 72.2\% of the scenarios.
Furthermore, the method demonstrated high temporal efficiency, locating final solutions in an average of 21.1 seconds.
These results validate the effectiveness of the constructed algorithmic components and endorse Automatic Algorithm Design as a robust alternative to manual heuristic tuning for complex scheduling problems.
\end{abstract}
